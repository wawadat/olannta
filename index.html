The Studio Donegal Range

Six distinctive Irish wools, each with unique character. From classic tweeds to premium blends, all crafted with generational textile heritage in Kilcar, Co. Donegal.

Soft Donegal

Our most popular merino blend, combining 100% merino softness with traditional woollen-spun technique. Lightweight and airy with large multicolored tweed flecks throughout. Strong 2-ply twisted structure creates cohesive, durable yarn perfect for colorwork projects. Soft enough for base layers, hats, and scarves yet structured for serious knitting. Blocking further softens the feel, creating a textile that looks beautifully rustic while feeling luxuriously soft. Ideal for knitters with sensitive skin.

A unique blend of 60% finest Irish fleece and 40% New Zealand wool, creating a rustic yet versatile yarn. Not tweedy like other ranges, this yarn brings warmth and breathability to outerwear and home accessories. Versatile, comfortable, and cosy in finished projects.

Our Story

The name Olannta: In Irish Gaelic, olann means wool. Adding the suffix -ta creates an evocative descriptor: olannta—meaning “woollen,” “of the wool,” or “brought into being.” It also honors Anna, intertwining the wild, natural fleece of the Atlantic coastline with the human touch that guides it across Europe.

The business: Olannta was founded to create an authentic, direct B2B bridge connecting the master weavers and spinners of Studio Donegal in Kilcar with independent yarn studios, designers, and mills across the European continent. Starting with focused, small-scale pilot distributions in Hungary and Romania, Olannta eliminates international supply barriers through dependable single-market logistics and verified provenance.
